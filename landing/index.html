Try it

Two minutes.

  1. 1 Clone and build
  2. 2 Hold Ctrl Space, say a name and a message
git clone https://github.com/Rishet11/voicy.git
cd voicy
./build.sh
open dist/Voicy.app

Needs desktop app installed where you send messages. Check: ./dist/Voicy.app/Contents/MacOS/Voicy --selftest

After you open it

  1. Find voicy in menu bar
  2. Allow Microphone and Contacts
  3. Hold, speak, release, press Enter

Works where you type. You stay in your app.

Questions.

Will it get me banned?

No. We open the app with the link it publishes for the web. No private API.

Does audio leave this device?

Never. It runs here. We make zero network calls and store no messages.

What if it hears the name wrong?

It will. We match by sound and spelling. Teach it once and it sticks.

Why not fix my words?

Because your voice is yours. We copy. We do not rewrite.

Hold a key. Say it. It's sent.
